About & Principles

§ 01

I am a software developer with over two years of hands-on production experience architecting web and mobile platforms on the MERN / Next.js stack, alongside high-concurrency backend services in Python.

I completed my Integrated B.Tech + MBA in Information Technology from ABV-IIITM Gwalior. Having both an engineering foundation and product-management training means I don't build software in a vacuum—I design schemas, API boundaries, and workflows around the fundamental business bottlenecks they exist to solve.

Outside of production sprints, my obsession is eliminating tedious manual overhead. Whether it's bridging cellular Android voice calls over ADB, parsing non-standard bank statements with multimodal LLMs, or automating government procurement paperwork down to 1-click A4 PDFs, I build deterministic systems that remove human error and save hundreds of operational hours.

TENET I
Zero-Busywork Engineering

If humans are repeatedly copying numbers, generating identical procurement letters, or dialing repetitive calls, we can build a deterministic, automated system to do it faster and without errors.

TENET II
Local-First & Network Resilience

Network latency spikes and connections drop; mission-critical operations shouldn't. Systems should cache proactively, leverage IndexedDB / SQLite, and support offline-first continuity.

TENET III
Tactile Polish & Sub-200ms Response

APIs are profiled for low latency, databases are indexed intentionally, and interfaces must feel responsive on hardware—active states scale, animations stay under 200ms, and frames hit 60fps.
